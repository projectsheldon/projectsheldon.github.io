Sale Webhook — automatic delivery

One-time setup, then zero typing. Paste the URL into your shop (Sellix / SellApp / Shopify webhooks) or your DM bot — every paid sale is reported automatically, and when your customer's payment and your wholesale payment both verify, Plan B keys land instantly. What my shop must send (payload + signature) 
POST {webhook_url}
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "sale_id": "unique per sale, e.g. your order number",
  "customer_discord": "buyer discord id",
  "product_key": "exact product key from catalog",
  "quantity": 1,
  "provider": "sellix | stripe | paypal | crypto | manual",
  "provider_tx_id": "payment id from your provider",
  "amount": 19.99,
  "currency": "EUR",
  "timestamp": 1730000000000,
  "signature": "HMAC_SHA256_hex(secret, sale_id.provider_tx_id.amount)"
}

Signature examples — Python: hmac.new(secret.encode(), f"{sale_id}.{tx}.{amount}".encode(), hashlib.sha256).hexdigest() · Node: crypto.createHmac("sha256", secret).update(`${sale_id}.${tx}.${amount}`).digest("hex")
